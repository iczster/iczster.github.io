## 🎭 Together in Electric Dreams 25th July 2025

We arrived at the **North West Computer Museum in Leigh** on 25th July 2025, excited for a proper **retro night out**. The **free buffet** at the start was welcome a bit of arcade gaming beforehand made us slightly late for the play, but the early arcade time was worth it. Air conditioning was much needed; it was **boiling hot** inside by the time the show started!

---

## 📺 The Play Experience

**Together in Electric Dreams** was **fantastic**. The play was **interwoven with media**, making use of screens to bring additional layers to the story. It was engaging, funny, and nostalgic, especially for those who remember the original film and era. The limited seating made it feel more intimate, even if we had to squeeze in at the back.

---

## 🕹️ Retro Gaming Fun

After the show, we wandered around the **museum**, which was a real treat. Playing **Arcadians** on the BBC Micro was a particular highlight proper **retro vibes** that brought back memories and made it feel like stepping into the 1980s. There was a lot to explore, and the combination of theatre and museum made for a unique, immersive night.

---

## 🎉 Final Thoughts

All in all, it was **an excellent evening**:  

- Great **performance and media integration** during the play  
- Fun **arcade and BBC gaming**  
- Nostalgic experience for fans of retro tech and 80s culture  
- A proper night out that blended theatre, gaming, and museum exhibits  

Definitely a **recommendation** for anyone in the North West looking for something quirky, engaging, and full of nostalgia.
