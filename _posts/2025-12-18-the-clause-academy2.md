# 🎸 The Clause – Manchester Academy 2  
**Wednesday 18th December 2025**

We got down to **Manchester Academy 2** nice and early for this one, partly to grab a decent spot and partly to catch the support. It turned out to be a good call.

---

## 🎶 Support: Kairos

First up were **Kairos**, a sharp guitar band from **Liverpool**  which I suppose I’ll forgive them for.

Joking aside, they were **really good**: tight, confident and clearly enjoying themselves. The guitars were on point and they warmed the room up properly. The only downside? They **didn’t play my favourite track, _Teetotal_**, which definitely cost them a few bonus points in my book.

Still, a strong opener and one worth keeping an eye on. I've been a fan for some time. 

---

## 🔥 The Clause: Full Throttle Energy

Then it was time for **The Clause**, a band I’m a **big fan of**, and they didn’t disappoint.

From the moment they came on, the room was **alive**.  
Big riffs, catchy hooks and a set packed with **banging tunes** the kind that instantly get a crowd moving. The place was bouncing from **front to back**, with that proper Academy atmosphere where everyone’s locked in and singing along.

There’s something reassuring about seeing nights like this **guitar bands are very much alive and kicking**, and The Clause are right at the sharp end of it.

---

## 🕰️ An Early Exit (Sadly)

It’s been a **tough week**, so we ducked out a little earlier than planned and didn’t quite make it to the end of the set. Even so, what we caught was more than enough to remind me why I like this band so much.

No regrets just unfinished business.

---

## 🎟️ Looking Ahead

The good news?  
We’ll be **catching them again in Bridlington in April**, and I’m already looking forward to it. If this Manchester show is anything to go by, it’ll be another belter.

---

## 🎤 Final Thoughts

A great night, strong support, and a headline set that absolutely delivered.  
Loud and full of life exactly what a gig should be.

More of this, please.

## 🎵 Setlist Highlights

Songs played included (not necessarily in order):

- Nothing’s As It Seems  
- In My Element  
- Exception  
- Never Ending Affair  
- Elisha  
- Fever Dream  
- Time of Our Lives  
- Fake It  

These tracks, both fan favourites and newer material, kept the crowd going throughout the set and showcased why The Clause continue to draw such enthusiastic crowds.
